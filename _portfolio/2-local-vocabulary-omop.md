## Motivation
Many institutions and countries use local coding systems that are not part of the standard OMOP vocabularies, which limits participation in OHDSI network studies.

## What I did
- Developed a **semi-automated framework** for standardizing Korean EDI codes
- Wrote a **step-by-step tutorial** for integrating local vocabularies into the OMOP CDM

## Outputs
- **Park, Y.**, *et al.* (2026). Integrating local vocabulary into the OMOP Common Data Model: A step-by-step tutorial. *Healthcare Informatics Research*, 32(4), 398–403. [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.4258/hir.2026.32.4.398)
- Oral presentation, OHDSI Global Symposium 2024 (Vocabulary Working Group)
- Presentations at KOSMI Fall 2024 and IIDH, Yonsei University Health System (2024)
