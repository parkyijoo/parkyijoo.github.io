**Master's thesis** — *Development and Application of a Standardized Common Data Model for Medical Device Post-Market Surveillance*

## Motivation
Medical devices are hard to study with routinely collected health data: device-level identifiers are rarely captured in a standardized way, which makes it difficult to compare specific devices across institutions after they reach the market.

## What I did
- Explored how **Unique Device Identifier (UDI)** codes can be represented in the OMOP CDM for device-level comparative analyses
- Built a **multi-institutional medical device CDM (MDV-CDM)** that extends the OMOP CDM for post-market surveillance
- Applied it to real-world comparative safety and effectiveness studies

## Applications
- **Transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR):** TAVR vs. surgical AVR, and balloon-expandable vs. self-expanding valves
- **Biliary stents:** metal vs. plastic stents in patients with pancreatic cancer
