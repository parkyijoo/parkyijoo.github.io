I am a Master's student in the **Department of Biomedical Systems Informatics at Yonsei University**, advised by Prof. Seng Chan You. I will graduate in **February 2027**.

My research focuses on generating reliable real-world evidence from observational health data. I work with the **OMOP Common Data Model (CDM)** and the **OHDSI** open-source ecosystem, from standardizing local vocabularies and medical device data to running comparative safety and effectiveness studies.

My Master's thesis, *Development and Application of a Standardized Common Data Model for Medical Device Post-Market Surveillance*, builds a multi-institutional medical device CDM and applies it to real-world comparisons of TAVR valves and biliary stents.

I am open to research opportunities in observational health research and medical informatics.

Research Interests
======
- **Observational studies & real-world evidence** — comparative safety and effectiveness, pharmacoepidemiology
- **Medical device post-market surveillance** — device-level evidence using UDI and CDMs
- **Data standardization** — OMOP CDM, standard and local vocabularies, ETL
- **Open science with OHDSI** — reproducible, multi-institutional network research
